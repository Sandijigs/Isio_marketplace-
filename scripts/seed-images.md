# Seed images

The demo catalog (`src/services/catalog/demo-seed.ts`) uses only the photos in `src/assets/demo/`. On seeding, each one is resized to at most 2000px wide, stripped of metadata and stored as `demo-<name>.jpg` in the image store.

These photos are used with permission for the demo. They are not covered by the MIT licence; their rights stay with their owners. The makers, towns and listing text attached to them in the seed are fictional and labelled as demo stalls on the site.
